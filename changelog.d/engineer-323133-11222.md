### Added

- The swarm pillar documents the lane model check on swarm creation: `swarm init` and `create` with a template
  refuse a lane model that is not `auto` and that neither the claude nor the codex catalog lists, with the
  message `swarm set` gives, before the ledger or swarm config is stored. It covers the accepted values, the
  catalog sources and their one hour Redis cache, a mistyped template example with its refusals, what
  `save-template`, `templates` and `restore` leave unchecked, and troubleshooting. The `swarm init`, `create`,
  lane `set` and `save-template` rows link it.
