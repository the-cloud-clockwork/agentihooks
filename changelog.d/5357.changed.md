`wt.sh new` and `wt.sh tmp` evict the oldest safe worktrees through `agentihooks gc --make-room` before refusing on the free space floor or the per repository cap. `agentihooks gc` removes a worktree untouched for `WT_STALE_DAYS` days (default 7) whatever holds it except a protected branch or a live owner, and removes a scratch repository worktree whose wip push fails after keeping its snapshot on a local wip branch, instead of keeping the worktree forever. The hive chart passes the floor, cap and stale days through a worktrees ConfigMap.
