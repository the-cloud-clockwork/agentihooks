### Added

- Autoscale holds a reserved share of seats for the review lane while review tasks wait: one seat above the live reviewers, or the `review_share` percent of the total when larger, never more than the waiting review tasks or the free seats of the review lane's harnesses. With no review work the share goes back to the other lanes and the configured `max_review` stays. Set it with `agentihooks swarm <slug> set review-share=N` (0 to 100, default 20). A review lane pinned to Codex with its model and effort stays on Codex.
