### Fixed

- With NATS unreachable, a bus open that does not reconnect tries twice within seconds and logs no traceback per attempt, so the ledger server's relay interval meets its stop deadline on reload.
- The ledger server prunes sent outbox rows and caps unsent rows on their own interval on every bus backend, so the outbox stays bounded with no reachable bus or on the memory bus. Unsent rows past the cap are dropped oldest first, and each drop is logged.
