### Added

- The tick engine can run a group's registered steps in legacy order, each in a copied context under its own budget deadline; a step that raises, exits or overruns is recorded as failed or late and never stops the next. The tick does not call it yet. Each engine step's record in the tick steps stream carries its budget and reads `late` when it overran; a late step is not counted among the pass's failed steps. `agentihooks swarm <slug> set tick-mode=sequential|parallel` stores the tick mode on the swarm config, sequential by default; no tick reads it yet.
