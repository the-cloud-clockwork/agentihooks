### Changed

- Hive members bind the work queues, streams and state buckets the hub created instead of creating them: the hive env sets `AGENTIHOOKS_BUS_BIND=1` beside the member credential, `agentihooks swarm spoke` always binds, and the hub creates the spoke buckets at start.
- A member credential minted by hive serve carries an explicit publish allow list with no stream or durable consumer create grant, and subscribes only to `_INBOX.>`.
