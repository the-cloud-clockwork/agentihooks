### Fixed

The dependency audit runs pip-audit through uvx with `--constraints .github/ci-tools/audit.lock`, which pins pip-audit and its whole closure, instead of excluding one platformdirs release, so a new upstream release can no longer break the lint audit. The lint job's audit step no longer writes its own `UV_CONSTRAINT` file. The Semgrep incident rule `uvx-call-without-constraint` refuses a uvx call that carries no constraint file.
