### Changed

- The tick launch cluster moves verbatim out of the swarm tick module: the launch check and acknowledgement helpers (`_verify`, `_launch_checks`, `_failed_hive_launch`, `_failed_launch`, `_retire_launch`, `_acknowledging`, `_expire_launch`, `_finished_launch`, `_collect_acks`) into `scripts.tick.steps.observe`, and the spawn outcome and admission helpers (`Started`, `_started`, `Failed`, `_spawn_failed`, `_not_launched`, `_failed_room_wait`, `_refused_reservation`, `_room_refused`, `_room_wait`, `_admission`, `_held_back`, `_settle_admission`, `_release_admission`) into `scripts.tick.steps.place`.
