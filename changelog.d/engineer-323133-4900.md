### Added

- Per record fencing keys: once a swarm is migrated with `python -m scripts.swarm.records_migration <slug> --writers-upgraded`, every fenced write sends only the records it changed through one Lua script that checks the lease owner, epoch and expiry and every expected record revision, then writes all of them or none, and appends one entry to a capped fence log stream. One agent change on the live swarm drops from about 2 MB to about 7 KB sent to Redis.
- The records port keeps the whole fencing document behind the same interface for bus deployments and swarms not yet migrated; readers use records when a swarm is migrated and the document otherwise.
