### Fixed

- The CI lab scheduler's metrics port answers a scrape from the last load
  snapshot its serve loop built instead of building a full load per request,
  which took minutes on a long queue and left `ci_lab_slots_wanted` without
  samples for the worker autoscaler. One build runs at a time, started once the
  last build started `REFRESH_MS` (15 seconds) ago; a failed build keeps the
  last snapshot. The new gauge `ci_lab_load_age_seconds` reports the served
  snapshot's age. Port 8800 still builds a load per `/capacity` request.
