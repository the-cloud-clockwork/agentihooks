### Fixed

- The CI lab scheduler's metrics port answers a scrape from the last load snapshot it built
  instead of building a full load for every request, which at production size outlasted the
  Prometheus scrape. The port's serve loop rebuilds the snapshot in the background once the
  last build started 15 seconds ago, a failed build keeps the last snapshot, and the new gauge
  `ci_lab_load_age_seconds` gives the age of the snapshot a scrape read.
