### Added

- An expert lineage ends on a mission proof. The expert that works it runs `agentihooks swarm <id> expert complete <proof file>`: the proof names every deliverable of the saved mission as proven, holds evidence under Validation, Smoke tests, Regression tests, Semgrep rules and Feature map, labels nothing a hypothesis, leaves no open item and links only merged pull requests. A proof with a fault is refused with each fault named; one that passes ends the lineage as `complete`, saves the proof, writes the seat recap, finishes the expert and tells the master. The master and the operator read it with `agentihooks swarm <id> expert proof <lineage>`.
- The `expert-stop` gate refuses the end of an expert's turn while its lineage runs, unless the expert handed its seat off or holds a declared wait. It ships at `deny`, is set with `agentihooks swarm <id> set expert-stop-gate=...` and shows in `swarm status` and on the ledger page with the other gates.

### Changed

- `agentihooks swarm <id> expert status <lineage>` names the seat's expert only while the lineage runs.
