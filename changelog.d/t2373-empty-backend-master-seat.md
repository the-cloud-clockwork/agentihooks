An inbox item addressed to an empty backend master seat now goes straight to the swarm's front door seat at send, where the master pool hands it to a live master, instead of waiting a full wake and escalation cycle in the empty seat. `MasterPool.empty` judges a seat empty when it was retired, when its registry occupant is finished or retiring or has left it, or when it has no occupant and no live master record names it. A backend master frozen at a prompt still holds its seat; the wake pass moves its items.
