`agentihooks scout "<question>" [--until] [--rounds] [--tier] [--harness] [--out]` answers a question with an evidence set: it runs read only scouter rounds through the headless runner on the caller's harness, merges items by source and excerpt, and after each round asks the classifier whether the set meets the until condition. A yes ends the run; an unavailable classifier runs to the round cap (default 3).
