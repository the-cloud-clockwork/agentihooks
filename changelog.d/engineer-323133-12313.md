### Changed

- Lab scheduler load builds read the grade revisions hash once and fetch demand records only for grades whose revision moved or that are not cached, in one pipeline that opens no transaction; a grade with no record is read from its document. No build downloads or hashes the document of a grade with a record. `python -m scripts.ci_lab.load_measure` also prints `documents_fetched` and `record_bytes_read` for each build.
