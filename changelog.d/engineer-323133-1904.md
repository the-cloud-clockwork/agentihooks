### Changed

- A hive member's Redis user writes only below its own hive key. Its inbox,
  delivery owners, seen marks and its agents' session readings (heartbeat,
  prompt, session model, progress, waits, stop blocks) live there; the
  controller reads them below the member each execution record places an agent
  on, and sends to an address a member runs land in that member's keys.
- Mail a member sends to any other address waits in its own keys until the
  swarm tick relays it; an item whose sender the member does not run is refused.
- The swarm tick refreshes live launch slot rows from every live hive's
  sessions report, so member executions keep their rows without a member write.
