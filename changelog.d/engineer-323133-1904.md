### Changed

- A hive member's Redis user writes only below its own hive key. Its inbox,
  delivery owners, seen marks, master pool and its agents' session readings
  (heartbeat, prompt, session model, progress, waits, stop blocks) live there.
  The controller reads an agent's session readings below the member its
  execution record places it on, and sends to an address a member runs land in
  that member's inbox keys.
- Mail a member sends to any other address waits in its own keys until the
  swarm tick relays it; an item whose sender the member does not run is refused.
- The swarm tick keeps live launch slot rows alive for two ticks from every live
  hive's sessions report, counting only executions placed on that hive, so
  member executions keep their rows without a member write.
