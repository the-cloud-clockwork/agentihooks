### Added

- Mutation stats collection keeps a flake ledger. Every stats bucket run loads `tests.flake_ledger` and points `CI_FLAKES` at a flakes file beside the bucket output, so a test that fails its first attempt and passes its rerun leaves a row with its first failure message and traceback, and the mutation stats job log prints it under `Retry failure reports`. Each collected part folder holds its buckets' rows in `flakes-<part>.jsonl`, which the part loader skips; stats cache records and keys stay unchanged, and the caller's `CI_FLAKES` is restored after each bucket.
