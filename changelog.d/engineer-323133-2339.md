### Added

- `agentihooks hive invite <label>` mints a hive member and writes its credential, ledger address and ledger CA into the OpenBao record a Kubernetes hive mounts; `--like` copies the addresses from a working hive.
- The swarm chart's `hiveOnly: true` renders only the worker hive, so each hive installs as its own release into its own namespace.
- `scripts/packaging/hive-reproduce.sh` and the Reproducible hive runbook time a new hive from its invite to the first engineer its join report grades healthy.
