`agentihooks swarm <id> master-drill` runs the master drill on a running or paused scratch proof swarm: it retires the front door master through the runtime, leaving the seat empty, sets the `nightwatch` gate to `enforce` and passes the tick's night watch once a minute on a drill clock that starts thirty minutes back. It prints and stores, under the swarm's `master-drill` key, the minute a successor held the front door seat and that successor's route; `recovered` is true only for a claude ai login successor inside the thirty minutes. The promotion swarm smoke runs the same drill in the built swarm image with a stand-in claude on the claude ai login route and fails unless it recovers.
