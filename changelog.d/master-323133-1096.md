## Added

The quick swarm master's seed and cadence block now tell the master to never trust that a pane message reached its seat without reading the pane back and confirming delivery, falling back to the room on NOT SENT, and to load a new `quick-swarm-master` skill covering how the swarm is built, how a seat launches and registers, how messages travel, how to troubleshoot a seat, and how to force a handoff, before any other action. Every seat's seed and cadence block also now carry a merge request discipline: pack each delivery into as few merge or pull requests as possible, one per repository, at most 20 changed files each, and an engineer asks the master before opening a new one.
