### Changed

- Every registered tick step declares its budget, the tick report p95 over sixty passes with half again as headroom in whole seconds, and its interval from the plan's interval table; a step with no declared timing is refused.
- The tick engine waits on a step for at most its budget: a step still running then is recorded late and left behind, and the pass goes on, so a 300 second step before spawn no longer delays spawn.
- Spawn needs only the judge steps, orphans and preempt, and the registry computes from the budgets that it starts by forty seconds into the pass.
