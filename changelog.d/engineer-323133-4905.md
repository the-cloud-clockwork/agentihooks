### Changed

- Every registered tick step declares its budget, the tick report p95 over sixty passes with half again as headroom in whole seconds, and its interval from the plan's interval table; a step with no declared timing is refused.
- The tick engine waits on a step for at most its budget: a step still running then is recorded late and left behind, and the pass goes on, so a 300 second step before spawn no longer delays spawn.
- Spawn needs only the judge steps, orphans and preempt, and the registry computes from the budgets that it starts by forty seconds into the pass once steps run by their needs; groups still run in today's order until the eager executor lands.
- A step left running past its budget keeps running in its own thread and its result is dropped.
- A step with no row in the tick report gets a ten second p95 until it is measured.
