### Changed

- The master claude ai login is one machine wide setting, `AGENTIHOOKS_MASTER_CLAUDE_LOGIN` (off by default), for the front door master of every swarm, on one home under `$AGENTIHOOKS_HOME/master-login`. `agentihooks claude login --master` takes no slug, and several swarms' masters share the login at once.

### Removed

- The per swarm `master-claude-login` setting of `agentihooks swarm set`. A swarm config saved with it still loads; run `agentihooks claude login --master` once after the upgrade, since the per swarm homes and recovery copies are no longer read.
