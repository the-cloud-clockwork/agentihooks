### Added

- The `flake-budget` job exports the lab flake records into `~/flake-reports/lab` beside the Actions reports, using a read only lab store credential from the `CI_LAB_FLAKES_*` Actions secrets. When a setting is unset, or the install or export fails, it prints one `Lab flakes` warning and the Actions reports still grade. The job stays red only when no source left a report. The budget names the source of each run, `lab` or `actions`, in `flake-budget.json` and in its summary.
