### Added

- A session started on an interactive login records its account in its quota
  snapshot, and swarm capacity reads the freshest snapshot for a declared
  tokenless Claude master account, so the master row shows its five hour and
  week left and the master reservation can close it at its weekly ceiling. A
  stale snapshot keeps the unknown row.
- `agentihooks balance` closes the Claude and Codex rows the master
  reservation closes, as the swarm quota view does, marks them `(reserved)`
  and names the reason under the table. `agentihooks quota --json` rows carry
  the reason in a new `reserved` field.
