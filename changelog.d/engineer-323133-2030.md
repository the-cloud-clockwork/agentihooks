### Added

- `agentihooks init` copies the hosted ledger's CA certificate from OpenBao (`k8s/agentihooks-swarm-ledger-ca`, field `ca.crt`) to `ledger-ca.pem` in the agentihooks home. Ledger clients without `LEDGER_CA_PEM` trust that file, or the one `LEDGER_CA_FILE` names, beside the system roots.
- With `ledger.tls.secretName` set, the ledger StatefulSet carries the Reloader annotation for that secret, so a renewed certificate rolls the ledger pod.
