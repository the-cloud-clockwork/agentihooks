### Added

- Every lab scheduler save of a live grade now stores the grade's next revision in the `agentihooks-ci:revisions` hash and its demand record (revision, tier, hits and job ids) in the `agentihooks-ci:records` hash, in one transaction with the grade document that retries while another writer changes the grade. Compaction bumps the revision and rewrites the record of each grade it strips. A grade that completes or is forgotten leaves the live set with neither field, and a closed grade's save removes both in the transaction that writes its document.
