### Changed

- A workstation whose swarms are all served in Anton or stopped no longer runs swarm work: the machine wide `agentihooks swarm tick` reconcile cancels the scheduled tick cron and removes the `agentihooks-swarm-waits`, `agentihooks-tick-watch` and `agentihooks-inbox-waker` units, keeping the scheduler and quota prober. Starting a local swarm brings them back.

### Added

- `agentihooks swarm <id> tick` runs one tick pass for a local swarm and exits 1 for a swarm served in Anton.
