### Added

- Every release published by the promotion gets one explanation of 20 to 100
  words per merged change, written by the gateway's Sonnet model and checked by
  code. The `Changelog` workflow keeps them newest first in `RELEASES.md` on the
  `changelog` branch, under the release tag and date with each change's date,
  merge commit, pull request and author, and adds them to the release notes.
