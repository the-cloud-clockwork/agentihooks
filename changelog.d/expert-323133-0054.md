### Fixed

- A CI lab plant pull request grade posts its `required` check as success when every gate it grades passes. Its plan carried the job `unit` while its select scope said `none`, and the required gate rules refuse a job the scope does not require, so every plant grade posted `required: failure` and no plant twin could be green. The plan is now a run of the full suite: scope `full` and the jobs that scope requires, `split`, `unit` and `shard-check`; a failed test unit or static gate still posts failure.
