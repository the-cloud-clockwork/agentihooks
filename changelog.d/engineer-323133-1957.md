### Changed

- The operator terminal setup refresh and the herdr sync of hive workers run on
  the agentihooks scheduler, every fifteen minutes and every fifteen seconds.
  `agentihooks swarm-terminal install` removes the old
  `agentihooks-swarm-terminal` timer and its two services, so the next
  `agentihooks deps ensure` after an upgrade moves the jobs. `swarm-terminal sync`
  runs one pass.

### Added

- `Scheduler.interval` schedules a job that repeats a fixed number of seconds
  after each dispatch.
