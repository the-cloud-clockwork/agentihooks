### Added

- `agentihooks swarm <id> expert template` prints the mission template an expert lineage is started from: the operator's words, the problem, the evidence, the numbered deliverables and how to report.
- `agentihooks swarm <id> expert start <mission file> [--effort xhigh|max]` lets the master or the operator save a mission in the swarm store under its sha256 and record a running lineage with its own seat. A mission that does not follow the template is refused with every fault listed.
- `agentihooks swarm <id> expert status`, `expert stop <lineage> --note` and `expert mission` show where each lineage stands, end one with a note, and print a lineage's saved mission. An expert reads the mission of its own lineage only.
- A handoff Read first entry may name a saved mission as `mission:<swarm>/<sha256>`.
