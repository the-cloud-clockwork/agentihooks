Optional master claude ai login: with the swarm setting master-claude-login on (off by default), the Claude front door master starts and resumes from a private home with its own login, forceLoginMethod claudeai and Remote Control under its seat name; agentihooks claude login --master provisions it, native Claude refreshes it and OpenBao keeps a recovery copy. Swarm status and the ledger master row show Remote Control on or why it is off, and a master Remote Control failure notice raises a master-rc health finding. With the setting off every launch is unchanged.
