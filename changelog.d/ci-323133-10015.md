### Fixed

- The Semgrep workflow runs semgrep-core on the Eio posix backend (`EIO_BACKEND: posix`), so its scan, feature-tests and charts jobs open no io_uring ring. Linux 6.14 and later charge each ring and registered buffer to the user's locked memory, and every Anton runner process on a node shares one 8 MiB limit as uid 1001, so semgrep failed at random with `Cannot allocate memory io_uring_queue_init`.
