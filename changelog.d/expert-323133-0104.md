### Fixed

- The CI lab scheduler no longer exits when it grades again a head whose grade it forgot earlier in the same
  process, such as a pull request closed and reopened on the same head and base. Before the post of a check the
  grade state holds no check run for, `Scheduler._post` has the poster forget what it remembers of that check run,
  so the grade opens new check runs, as a restarted scheduler does, instead of meeting the statuses of the
  forgotten grade.
