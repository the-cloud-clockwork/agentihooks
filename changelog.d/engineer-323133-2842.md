### Added

- `hive serve` with `AGENTIHOOKS_NATS_SIGNER` set to an OpenBao record (operator and account seeds, a system account credential and the NATS URL) adds a signed NATS user credential to every refresh answer. `hive refresh` saves it as `hive.creds` and points `AGENTIHOOKS_BUS_CREDENTIALS` at it. `hive revoke` and `hive remove` revoke that credential by pushing a re-signed account JWT.
- `AGENTIHOOKS_BUS_CREDENTIALS` gives the NATS bus its credential: either a creds file path, or `openbao:<record>`, which reads the record's `credentials` field. This is how hub and ledger role credentials come from OpenBao.
