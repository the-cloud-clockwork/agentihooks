### Changed

- The dispatcher never moves seats between lanes, replacing the earlier lane
  split entry. The tick runs no lane split, the `seat_wait` bottleneck remedy
  raises its fix task or proposes one instead of moving a seat, and autoscale
  ignores any stored lane shift, so ceilings follow live agents, quota seats,
  host room and ready work only. Lane caps change only when the operator or the
  master sets them through `swarm set`, the ledger page or the `create` and
  `doctor start` flags; `lane-split-gate` is accepted and moves nothing.
