### Changed

- The swarm planner skill says how a planner finishes a plan task that enhances one thin or vague task. It writes and publishes the plan section and adds no task. It points the target at the section with `task set`, then closes with `done --slice <target>`. The skill also names the review that follows: a clean slice check at full autonomy marks the target planned, at delegate the master runs `plan approve --task` or `plan send-back --task` with the plan task's id, and at manual and assist the operator decides.
