### Added

- `agentihooks swarm <slug> set masters-step=N` (12 for swarms made by
  `swarm create`, 0 for swarms created before it) sizes master seats
  as one plus the running engineer, CI and planner agents divided by N; the
  ledger page shows it beside the lane caps. `set masters=N` sets a manual
  count and the step to `0`, and an explicit `master-per` rule still wins.

### Changed

- The ten minute master downscale cool off restarts whenever the wanted count
  rises, and a rise withdraws the retire request of a seat wanted again.
