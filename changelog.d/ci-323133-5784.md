### Fixed

- A CI lab worker whose lease call gets no answer from the scheduler (refused, reset, cut short or timed out) or a server error answer now logs `ci-lab worker: lease call failed, retrying in <n>s` and leases again after a backoff that doubles from one second up to ten seconds, instead of exiting. A scheduler roll no longer restarts every worker container and drops its running units. A client error answer, such as a wrong scheduler URL, still ends the worker.
