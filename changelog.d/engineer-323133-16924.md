### Fixed

- Settled CI lab queue jobs expire six hours after they settle, with their
  head's done set, instead of staying in the swarm Redis forever; a retry or a
  requeue clears the expiry, and the scheduler drains the old global done set
  one batch per pass so the existing settled jobs expire too. Six hours outlives
  the longest gap measured between a job settling and its grade collecting it,
  under seven minutes, and the retention is the scheduler setting
  `Settings.retention_ms`.
