### Fixed

- `kubectl_mutation_guard` now matches CI Manifesto §2: bypass mode (`disable controls`) lifts the whole guard and logs the pass-through instead of blocking. `KUBECTL_MUTATION_GUARD_EXEC_ALLOW` lists comma-separated `namespace/pod-glob` entries whose `kubectl exec` or `kubectl cp` target pod is let through without bypass mode; a command naming an unknown flag before its target allowlists nothing.
