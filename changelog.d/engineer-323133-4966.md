### Added

- `AGENTIHOOKS_TICK_CADENCE` names the slow lane cadence in whole minutes, one to five. `scripts.tick.engine.cadence_s` refuses a cadence that breaks the delivery guard, heartbeat limit, lease TTL or stale watcher, and `interval_s` gives a slow lane step the larger of its interval and the cadence. No pass skips a step on its interval yet, so the setting is validated but does not change a pass today; the scheduler cron stays every minute.
