### Fixed

- The CI lab scheduler's release pass no longer lets a head stuck on its manifest fetch occupy one of the eight start places forever: a put-back head now counts only against a new `LOOKED_AT_PER_PASS` (24) cap on heads examined in the pass, and the loop moves on to the next longest-waiting head so the real `STARTS_PER_PASS` (8) budget still reaches heads behind a stuck one.
