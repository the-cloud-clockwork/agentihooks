### Added

- `swarm focus` and `swarm unfreeze` print the claim gate verdict of every task under the target, and refuse a target that does not exist.
- `agentihooks api --slug <slug> get focus` lists every focus record with each task's verdict and the minutes since the focus with no claim.
- A swarm health finding tells the master when a focused, claimable task stays unclaimed past three ticks, and the master loop reads the focus list every loop.
