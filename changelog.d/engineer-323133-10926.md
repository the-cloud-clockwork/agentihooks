### Added

- The hives pillar documents the saved account check on a hive spoke launch: a successor routes to the account saved
  in its relaunch assignment or handoff envelope only while the hub's account slot has a worker seat on it for the
  launching execution, otherwise the launch passes no route and normal launch routing picks the account. It covers
  the quota repick and front door exceptions, the `unavailable` refusal when the hub cannot answer, the incident that
  motivated it, checks, guards, limitations and troubleshooting. The load balancing and account login pages link to
  it.
