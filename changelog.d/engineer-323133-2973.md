### Added

- `agentihooks ledger --slug <slug> get tasks|phases|followups|priorities/<id>` prints one ledger item through the ledger API, and `agentihooks ledger --slug <slug> tasks [--state] [--lane] [--text]` lists open tasks from the tasks collection, neither loading the whole ledger.

### Changed

- The master opening prompt and the master role rule read the ledger one item at a time with those commands and forbid `ledger show`, which downloads the whole ledger and slows every write.
