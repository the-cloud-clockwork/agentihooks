### Changed

- The op context Protocol, its live check and its Semgrep rule widen from the ledger core's `EXTENSION_OPS`
  registry to every module the core dispatches an op to by name: the agent and artifact op families, the sync
  kinds and the thread ops, and the core itself, all discovered from the core's own source with no hand kept
  list. `OpContext` gains `dropped` and `unstored`, the two members the artifact ops use.
