### Added

- The swarm tick reads the cluster autoscaler status ConfigMap that `AGENTIHOOKS_AUTOSCALER_STATUS` names and raises one ledger alert to the masters when a node group fails to scale up twice in a row, a scale up times out or launched instances stay past the join budget, naming the node group, the count, the last failure and the ready nodes against the target. The alert closes once every node group recovered; an unset setting reads nothing.
