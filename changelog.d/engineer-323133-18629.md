### Fixed

- A plan task's hub admission builds its steering evidence from narrow ledger reads instead of the whole ledger
  export: the overview, its own phase, each phase it depends on with its comment thread, the tasks of those phases
  and, when its phase holds a published plan, the artifact rows. The steering text is unchanged, and the admission no
  longer spends its answer budget on the export.

### Added

- The ledger API tasks selection takes a `phases` filter, and the ledger client reads tasks by phase, one record's
  thread and the artifact rows.
