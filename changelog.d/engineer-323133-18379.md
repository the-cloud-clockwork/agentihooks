### Fixed

- A hive worker spawn now reads the Claude cap the hub admission reads: the session band scaled toward `AGENTIHOOKS_HIVE_SESSION_CEILING` (default 12), computed once by `session_bands.worker_cap` and read by the hive lane spokes and the hive order consumer through `capacity.worker_accounts`. The hub no longer admits a seat the hive spawn refuses. Interactive and single machine routing keep the plain band.
- A spawn refused as unavailable gives its claim back with the refusal, and the hub holds that task off that hive until the hive's reported caps or session counts change or an agent of the swarm that had started ends. Another task's refused give back lifts no hold. The hub no longer readmits the same task to the same hive after each refusal.
