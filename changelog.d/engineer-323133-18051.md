### Fixed

- `agentihooks ledger watch` keeps streaming when the shared ledger database drops its connection: at its start and mid stream it waits with a delay that doubles from `--interval` to 30 seconds and starts over after the next frame, then resumes from its cursor without printing an event twice. An HTTP 401, or a 403 or 404 whose body carries the ledger's `forbidden` or `ledger_missing` code, that repeats after the credential is read again prints `STOPPED ledger watch: <reason>` and exits 1; any other 403 or 404, server errors and lost connections keep retrying.
